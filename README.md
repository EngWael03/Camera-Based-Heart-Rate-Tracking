Camera-Based Heart Rate Tracking

Estimate heart rate from a video of exposed skin, using a regular camera.

How to Use
Open the project in PyCharm.
Record a video following the guidelines below.
Run the code on your video.
Adjust the box size if needed (see Adjusting the Box Size).
Video Guidelines

For the most accurate results:

Length: between 55 and 60 seconds.
Camera: a lower-resolution camera is preferred, as it helps reduce noise and artifacts.
Lighting: use as little artificial lighting as possible.
Body area: record a clear area of skin, such as the wrist, face, or neck.
Avoid hair in the recorded area.
Adjusting the Box Size

The box marks the area of the video that is analyzed. You can change its size to fit your video so that it covers only clear skin.
